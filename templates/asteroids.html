What does "Potentially Hazardous" mean?
NASA classifies an asteroid as Potentially Hazardous (PHA) when its orbit regularly comes within 0.05 AU of Earth (~7.5 million km) and it is estimated to be at least 140 m across. It does not mean a collision is expected — it means the orbit warrants ongoing monitoring.

What does "No Threat" mean?
The asteroid's current orbital path does not bring it close enough to Earth to meet the PHA threshold. Future impacts are considered negligible based on known data.
