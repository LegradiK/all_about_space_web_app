When will I get an alert?

ISS pass

  • About 30 minutes before the ISS appears.
  • It climbs at least 25° above the horizon and is bright (magnitude −1 or brighter).
  • It is sunlit while your sky is dark, so you can actually see it.
  • The forecast cloud cover for your city is 40% or less.

Aurora

  • Red AuroraWatch UK alert: anywhere in the UK.
  • Amber: northern England, Northern Ireland and Scotland.
  • Yellow: northern Scotland only.
  • Only when it is properly dark and cloud cover is 50% or less.
  • At most one alert a night, unless the storm gets stronger.

Data: ISS orbit from CelesTrak, aurora from AuroraWatch UK, weather from Open-Meteo.
